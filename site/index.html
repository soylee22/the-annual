05 / THE SMALL PRINT THAT MATTERS

Price is not execution.

Your confirmed fill was $1,557.71. The broker displays a rounded average of $1,557.72. Public exchange quotes value the shares here. They can differ from the broker’s OTC bid and the value you could actually sell for.

The benchmark is a model.

£1,000 converted at your confirmed entry FX buys hypothetical fractional SPY shares. The closest completed minute before entry sets the price. Dividends are modelled as reinvested. Benchmark fees and taxes are excluded.

Freshness is visible.

The publisher checks free quotes about every 20 minutes during weekday US trading windows. Rankings refresh daily. Provider delays and GitHub queues can intervene. Source times stay attached to retained data.
